# Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Universal Stock Advisor ("the app", "we") is run by Francois Kassis. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: francois.kassis@gmail.com.

## What we collect

- **Your account:** email address, name, a password (stored only as a secure hash) or your Google sign-in, and when you confirmed your email.
- **Your answers and settings:** the onboarding answers (experience, risk comfort, time horizon, interests), language, currency and email preferences.
- **What you do in the app:** your watchlist, price alerts, notifications, practice account (virtual money), the holdings you type into your portfolio, lesson and quiz progress, and your questions to the Copilot with its answers.
- **Technical data:** sign-in sessions (the device type and IP address of each session, kept to protect your account) and basic server logs.

We don't collect your bank or broker details, and the app never connects to them.

## Why we use it

- To run the app: show your markets, prices, alerts, practice account and portfolio, and answer your questions.
- To send emails you need (confirming your address, resetting your password, alerts and the morning brief if you turned them on).
- To keep the service safe and working (preventing abuse, fixing problems).

We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

## Who else is involved

- **The AI Copilot runs on our own server.** Your questions are not sent to an outside AI company.
- **Market data and news** come from outside sources (for example price and news providers). We request prices and headlines from them; we don't send them your personal data.
- **Web search** for the Copilot goes through our own search service, which queries public search engines with the words of your question, not with your identity.
- **Google**, only if you choose "Sign in with Google".
- **Email delivery** through our email provider, Google (Gmail).

## How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), your data is deleted, apart from what the law requires us to keep and backups, which are overwritten within 14 days.

## Your choices and rights

You can see and change most of your data in the app, turn email off in Profile, and delete your account at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or ask questions at francois.kassis@gmail.com. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under data-protection law.

## Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and access to our servers is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## Changes

We'll update this page when things change and show the date above. For important changes we'll tell you in the app or by email.
